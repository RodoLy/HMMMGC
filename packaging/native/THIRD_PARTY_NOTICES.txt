Third-party notices / Avisos de terceros
========================================

This package puts together free software from several authors. The game is NOT
included. The list says what each part is, where it is in the package, its
license and where its source is. The license texts are in the "licenses"
folder, or inside the folder of the part when it brings its own. The exact
versions are in VERSIONS.txt. The source of this project, and the patches it
applies to the parts below, is at https://github.com/RodoLy/HMMMGC; if
anything of that cannot be obtained there, ask for it by opening an issue.

Este paquete junta software libre de varios autores. El juego NO está incluido.
